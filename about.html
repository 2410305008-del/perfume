Our Craft

Patience, Alchemy, and Respect for Nature

Unlike commercial perfumes compounded with petrochemicals, our scents undergo a traditional 6-week slow maturation in chilled French oak casks. This gentle maceration marries the delicate top notes of citrus with deep base resins of sandalwood and amber.

Every bottle is individually inspected, filled by hand, and sealed with our signature gold emblem before journeying to your vanity.
